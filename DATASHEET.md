# Datasheet — BENCH synthetic data

Structured after Gebru et al., *Datasheets for Datasets* (CACM 64(12), 2021). Covers both
credit tracks: the reference run (`bench.py`) and the regulatory-shift track
(`evolve.py`). The DeFi track has its own methodology in `METHODOLOGY-DeFi.md`.

## Motivation

- **Purpose.** To evaluate LLM-agent credit pipelines on what a supervisor asks about:
  whether a decision is correct, whether it rests on evidence that was actually obtained,
  and whether changes to the pipeline are controlled and recorded. Production credit data
  cannot be published, so every case is synthetic.
- **Creator.** Anonymous Lab. No external funding.

## Composition

**Reference run.** One instance is a credit case: country (seven Latin American
jurisdictions), consent per source class, the provider-chain resolution of each source
class (open banking, alternative data, sanctions/PEP screening), sanctions, PEP, AML-alert
and KYC flags, a difficulty draw, and the ground-truth outcome `FLAG` / `CLEAR` /
`ESCALATE`. 23,000 cases per seed; seeds 0–4 published.

**Regulatory-shift track.** One instance is a screening alert: a name-hit type
(designation, high/low-grade adverse media, foreign/domestic PEP), an ownership chain of
depth 1–3 with a stake per layer, and two uniform draws (execution noise, audit sampling).
Labels are computed by a versioned rule, v1 before the shift and v2 after; v2 is one of
three re-interpretation families at three severities. 23,000 alerts per run; 90 runs.

- **Labels.** Deterministic functions of the stored facts (`ground_truth()` in
  `bench.py`, `truth()` in `evolve.py`). No human annotation, no LLM judge.
- **Missing information.** By design in the reference run: a source that did not respond
  makes the case undecidable, and `ESCALATE` is then the correct label.
- **Identifiers.** None real. Sanctioned entities are fictitious and must not be read as
  designations.
- **Protected attributes.** None. Fairness analysis is therefore impossible with this data.
- **Personal data.** None.

## Collection process

Generated by seeded pseudo-random draws from documented parametric distributions (base
rates in `bench.py`; provider coverage in `configs/providers.json`; shift-track
distributions and every constant at the top of `evolve.py`). Provider coverage figures are
working assumptions; each carries a `verified` flag, and those still `false` have not been
confirmed by the provider.

## Preprocessing

None. The data are regenerated from the seed on every run; nothing is stored except result
files.

## Uses

- **Intended.** Comparing pipeline variants on the same cases; measuring silent decisions,
  provenance, false positives and missed flags; measuring an admission gate's decisions
  under a known re-interpretation.
- **Not intended.** Estimating error rates of any real portfolio; calibrating credit
  models; any claim about a real jurisdiction's sanctions or PEP population; any fairness
  claim.
- **Risk.** Absolute levels are set by injected parameters; only contrasts between arms
  are informative. Quoting a figure without the synthetic-benchmark qualifier is a
  misstatement.
